How a project runs

Five steps. No surprises.

01

Listen

One call to understand the goal, the audience and the budget. We ask more than we pitch.

02

Plan

A written plan with scope, timeline and price. You know what you get before anything starts.

03

Make

Scripts, campaigns or code. Work moves across a shared board, so progress is visible every day.

04

Launch

Publish the video, switch on the campaign, release to the stores. We stay for launch day.

05

Grow

Measure, report in plain language, improve. Then do it again, a little better.

How we stay honest

Every video, task and call on one board.

We built our own workspace. Each video shows its stage, each task its owner, each call its outcome. Nothing depends on someone remembering.
